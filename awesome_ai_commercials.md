# 🎬 AI Video Commercial Generators

If you want to generate high-quality video commercials just by typing a text prompt, you are looking for **Text-to-Video Generative AI**. 

Depending on exactly how much control you want over the final product, here are the absolute best tools available right now for making commercials from a prompt:

---

## 1. The "Do It All For Me" Option (Script, Voice, Video, Music)
*If you just want a finished, ready-to-post commercial in 5 minutes, this is your best bet.*

*   **[InVideo AI](https://invideo.io/)**: This is hands-down the easiest way to make a commercial. You type a prompt like: *"Create a 60-second commercial for a local coffee shop named Bean Roasters, make it sound energetic, and use a female British voiceover."*
    *   **What it does:** The AI will write the marketing script, clone a realistic human voice to read the script, search through millions of premium stock videos (or generate its own) to match the words, add background music, and put the whole video together on an editing timeline for you.
    ```bash
    > SaaS Platform - No installation required.
    > Access via web browser at invideo.io
    ```

---

## 2. The Hollywood Director Option (Generate B-Roll Footage)
*If you want to direct the commercial shot-by-shot and generate the raw video files yourself to edit together in Premiere Pro or CapCut, use these tools.*

*   **[Runway Gen-3 Alpha](https://runwayml.com/)**: Runway is currently the industry leader available to the public. Their newest Gen-3 model creates photorealistic, cinematic video clips from text prompts. It is used by actual advertising agencies today for generating B-roll footage.
    ```bash
    > SaaS Platform - No installation required.
    > Access via web browser at runwayml.com
    ```
*   **[Luma Dream Machine](https://lumalabs.ai/dream-machine)**: This is the newest major competitor to hit the market. It is insanely fast and generates incredibly realistic 5-second video clips from text prompts. It is currently free to try!
    ```bash
    > SaaS Platform - No installation required.
    > Access via web browser at lumalabs.ai/dream-machine
    ```
*   **[Pika Labs](https://pika.art/)**: If your commercial is going for a 3D animated look, an anime style, or needs very specific camera movements (like *"Pan left"* or *"Zoom in"*), Pika is fantastic at following those specific cinematic instructions.
    ```bash
    > SaaS Platform / Discord Bot - No installation required.
    > Access via web browser at pika.art
    ```
